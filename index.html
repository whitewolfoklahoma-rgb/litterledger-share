Getting around

Start in Dogs with your dams and sires. Add a Litter when a breeding happens or puppies arrive, record its costs in the same tab, then track each puppy — weights, status, deposit, placement — in Puppies. The Money tab turns it all into cost per puppy, break-even price, and who still owes you what.

A sample litter is pre-loaded (one litter in progress, weights logged, two puppies placed) so you can see every screen working. Sample records are marked, and one button removes only the samples — your own records are never touched.

How LitterLedger works

The idea

One file, no account, no subscription — your kennel records never leave this device. Dogs first, then litters and their costs, then a record for every puppy: what it weighed, what it sold for, what's been paid, and who still owes you. At any moment the Money tab shows your true cost per puppy and the price below which a litter loses money.

Formulas used

Cost per puppy = the litter's total recorded costs ÷ puppies born alive in that litter. If a litter has no live-born count entered yet, the number of puppy records is used instead; if there are no puppies at all, cost per puppy shows $0 rather than a made-up number.

Break-even price per puppy = the same figure: the sale price at which one puppy's price exactly covers its share of the litter's costs. A puppy's own extra costs (entered on the puppy) come on top of its share — the Puppies tab shows each puppy's total cost (share + extras).

Collected per puppy = deposit received + balance paid so far. Balance due = sale price − collected. Only puppies marked Reserved or Placed appear on the outstanding-balances list — an Available puppy has no buyer yet, so nobody owes you anything for it.

Gross margin per litter = collected so far − total litter costs. Puppies you keep aren't sales; their price fields are ignored in the money totals unless you enter amounts on them.

Expected whelp date = mating date + the gestation-days setting (default 63). It is a planning estimate only.

Your data

Everything is stored in this browser's local storage on this device only — including buyer names and contacts, which is why they never leave it. Clearing your browser's site data erases it — export backups from Settings & Backup and keep the file somewhere safe. A backup from this app can be re-imported into any copy of LitterLedger.

Record-keeping only. LitterLedger is a notebook and calculator. It is not veterinary, medical, legal, or tax advice, and it makes no prediction or guarantee about breeding outcomes, puppy health, or prices. Health-testing entries are your own records of results you were given. For animal health questions, ask your veterinarian; for taxes and contracts, ask the relevant professional.
